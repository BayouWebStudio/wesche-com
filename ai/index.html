Confidentiality

Your AI won't leak your data.

A test looks for private records, files, or one customer's session showing up in an answer, a log, or someone else's chat.

Integrity

It can't be tricked into saying or doing the wrong thing.

A test tries prompt injection, jailbreaks, and instructions meant to override the bot, including attempts to reveal the system prompt.

Availability

It stays up when you need it.

A test looks for requests that stall the bot, trap an agent in a loop, or run usage up until the system is unusable.

A report says what was tried and what happened. It is a record of a test, not a guarantee and not a certification.

01 / Who it's for

Businesses rolling out AI without a security team.

Small and mid-size businesses putting an AI chatbot, a support bot, or an agent in front of customers or staff. The exposure is sharpest in healthcare, legal, finance, and real estate, where a leaked record or a wrong answer already has a cost you can name.

You do not need a security department to start. You do need to know what the system can see, and what it did when someone tried to make it misbehave.

Houston, Texas. Remote. Based in Houston. The testing and the write-up are done remote, on a staging system or a scoped production window you approve.
02 / Services

Four kinds of work.

Scope follows a look at the system. Prices are not listed here.

01

AI chatbot and agent security check

Red-team testing against the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications: prompt injection, data leaks, jailbreaks, and system-prompt leaks. You get a written report of what was tried, what failed, and what held. After you change the system, there is a retest.

02

Ongoing retesting

A model update or a prompt change can reopen something the last test closed. Retests run after those updates, with the same kind of written record.

03

Private, hardened local AI

Setups that keep data on your own hardware. The model runs on machines you control, with the serving stack hardened and the configuration written down. This is the same kind of local deployment work published in the lab, aimed at a business system.

04

Texas AI law and NIST readiness

Testing and documentation support for the Texas Responsible Artificial Intelligence Governance Act (TRAIGA, effective January 1, 2026) and the NIST AI Risk Management Framework. The result is a test record and a plain map of how the system is used.

Readiness and testing support only. It is not legal advice, and it is not a finding that you comply with the law.
