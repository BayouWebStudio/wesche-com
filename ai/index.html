Confidentiality

Customer data leaks.

A test looks for private records, files, or one customer's session showing up in an answer, a log, or someone else's chat.

Integrity

Promises you are on the hook for.

A test tries prompt injection, jailbreaks, and instructions that make the bot invent a policy, a price, or a refund.

Availability

It goes down when you need it.

A test looks for requests that stall the bot, trap an agent in a loop, or run usage up until the system is unusable.

A report says what was tried and what happened. It is a record of a test, not a guarantee and not a certification.

01 / Who it's for

Teams that own the code and ship often.

Agencies, dev shops, and startups that build custom AI chatbots and agents. You own the code, and a prompt or model change can ship the same day.

Startups running open-source models are the next fit, including teams that serve the model themselves.

If the bot is a vendor's widget and you do not control the code, the offer is a lighter AI configuration and exposure review, not a full audit.

02 / Services

Four kinds of work.

Every test runs under a signed written scope and your permission. Prices are not listed here.

01

AI chatbot and agent red-team audit

Testing against the OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications, using garak, PyRIT, and promptfoo, plus manual testing. The pass covers prompt injection, data leaks, jailbreaks, and system-prompt leaks. You get a written report of what was tried, what failed, and what held. After you change the system, there is a retest.

02

Ongoing retesting

A model update or a prompt change can reopen something the last test closed. Retests run after those updates, with the same kind of written record.

03

AI configuration and exposure review

For a business on a vendor's chatbot widget. A lighter review of how the bot is configured, what data it can reach, and what it is allowed to say or do. This is not a full red-team audit.

04

Private, hardened local AI

Setups that keep data on your own hardware. The model runs on machines you control, with the serving stack hardened and the configuration written down. This is the same kind of local deployment work published in the lab, aimed at a business system.
