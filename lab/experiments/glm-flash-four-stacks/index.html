Serving study / recorded October 1–3, 2026

GLM-5.3-Flash on two DGX Sparks: four serving stacks

Same model, same two Sparks, four ways to serve it: Kindling's vLLM with NVIDIA's NVFP4, Mia's vLLM kit with EXL3, and TensorFold 0.6.2 with EXL3 and with MLX 4-bit. Kindling wins crowds and long prompts. TensorFold MLX wins single-user speed and repeats, with byte-identical output. The best-looking volcano came from TensorFold EXL3, after a one-character fix.

Read before comparing

The setup, the caveats, the limits.

Hardware: two DGX Sparks (GB10, 128 GB each), tensor-parallel 2 over 200 Gb RoCE. All four stacks used an incoai DFlash2 drafter; that drafter is licensed CC BY-NC-ND.

Stacks: Kindling kindlingai/glm-5.3-flash-gx10 @ d5abfe1 on nvidia/GLM-5.3-Flash-NVFP4. Mia MiaAI-Lab/GLM-5.3-Flash-EXL3-2x-DGX-Sparks @ 6278ecb, published image, default profile. TensorFold 0.6.2 @ 56e2e3e (the latest release when run) with Mia-AiLab/GLM-5.3-Flash-EXL3-TR3-4bpw and with TensorFold/GLM-5.3-Flash-MLX-4bit-MTP.

What each comparison isolates: TensorFold EXL3 vs TensorFold MLX is the same engine with two quantizations. Kindling vs Mia is NVFP4 vs EXL3 on vLLM, but two different vLLM builds with their own patches, and Mia's kit pins a newer drafter revision. Kindling vs TensorFold mixes engine and format.

Exactness: TensorFold gave byte-identical replies run to run and alone versus 16 at once (8/8 both). Kindling repeated itself (8/8) but changed under load (0/8); Mia's kit varied in both (0/8, 0/8).

Incidents: a first Mia speed run was thrown out after two copies of the harness hit the same server; every Mia number here is from the clean rerun. TensorFold ignores the switch Kindling's test uses to turn thinking off, so its first thinking-off pass was still thinking; the published row uses the switch TensorFold honours and was checked to produce zero reasoning tokens.

Island prompt, verbatim: "Create an interactive tropical island weather simulator in Three.js. Let me switch between sunshine, rain, thunderstorms with lightning, and a hurricane. Make the island and surrounding ocean react to the weather, with smooth transitions and convincing motion. Include orbit and zoom controls. Make it mobile-friendly with touch controls, easy-to-tap weather buttons, a responsive layout, and smooth performance on phones. Make it visually impressive, and choose your own style and details. Deliver everything in one HTML file, with no external libraries besides Three.js and its official addons."

Sampling: temperature 1.0, top_p 0.95, thinking on, no output cap beyond the serving window. One generation per stack per prompt, so the visual ranks describe these runs, not the quantizations. Originals are published untouched; every repair is a separate copy with its diff.
